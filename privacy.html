Privacy policy

Effective 8 September 2026 · applies to saltigrade.com and the office’s internal finance application

Who we are

Saltigrade is a single-family office. Its software serves one household; there are no customers, no accounts for the public, and no advertising.

What we access

With the account owner’s consent, the office’s finance application connects to banks and custodians through Plaid and reads account balances, investment holdings, and transactions from the accounts the owner chooses to connect. Nothing is accessed beyond what powers the household’s own reporting.

How it is used

The data powers a private daily briefing and internal dashboards for the household. It is used for no other purpose.

Where it lives

Data is stored on hardware the office owns and operates. Beyond Plaid’s own service, no third-party cloud holds it.

Sharing

Never sold. Never shared. No analytics, no trackers — this website sets no cookies.

Your choices

Connected institutions can be reviewed or disconnected at any time through the Plaid Portal (my.plaid.com). Questions and requests: shehan@saltigrade.com.
